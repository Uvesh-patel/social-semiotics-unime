# Social Semiotics

Course materials from the **Social Semiotics** module, part of the Master's Degree in **Cognitive Science and Theory of Communication (LM-55/92)** at the **University of Messina**, Italy.

> **Academic Year: 2022-2023**

---

## About the Course

Social Semiotics is a branch of semiotics that focuses on how meaning is created and communicated in social contexts. It examines how semiotic resources—such as language, images, gestures, and sounds—are used to construct meaning in various cultural and social settings.

This course is part of the interdisciplinary Master's Degree in Cognitive Science and Theory of Communication, which combines cognitive psychology, neuroscience, linguistics, artificial intelligence, philosophy, and social sciences with advanced communication theories.
